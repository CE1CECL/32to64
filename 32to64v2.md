# 32to64v2

Hello Everyone!

Today, I will show you how to upgrade Windows Sever 2003, R1 or R2 (in this video, Windows Sever 2003 R2), 32-bit to 64-bit! (better known as `32to64v2`/`video 2`)

Here, for reference, are the ISOs I will be using for this video:
`en_win_srv_2003_r2_enterprise_with_sp2_vl_cd1_x13-48610.iso` `en_win_srv_2003_r2_enterprise_x64_with_sp2_vl_cd1_x13-48614.iso`
`en_win_srv_2003_r2_enterprise_with_sp2_vl_cd2_x13-68584.iso` `en_win_srv_2003_r2_enterprise_x64_with_sp2_vl_cd2_x13-68588.iso`

32-Bit ISO Product Key: `HDXR2-62748-6XG6Y-4BT6T-R6H43`
64-Bit ISO Product Key: `B76XF-FCR39-XBGFF-YMBFH-G6PMT`

Note: As of making this video, `64to32v2` does NOT currently work, therefore, only 32to64v2 will be shown in this video.

And also note this video does NOT work on Windows XP 32-bit to 64-bit, this only works on Windows Server 2003!

1. In the root of your System Drive, usually `C:\`, make a folder named `32to64v2`
2. In the `32to64v2` folder, make 2 folders, `32` & `64`.
3. Download (with a usable browser of some sort, like Firefox 52.9.0esr), [the upgrade helper script](http://ce1cecl.github.io/32to64/32to64v2.bat)
4. Save the upgrade helper script as `32to64v2.bat` under the `32to64v2` folder
5. As a user of the `Administrators` group on the Windows Sever 2003 device (hope you have been doing all of this as an `Administrator` this whole time, if not, here is your friendly reminder!), run the `32to64v2.bat` script!
6. In the `Windows Setup` window, make sure `Installation Type:` is set to `Upgrade (Recommended)`, and click `Next`. Tick `I accept this agreement`, and click `Next` again. 
7. Enter your 32-Bit ISO Product Key, in my case, I will type in: `HDXR2-62748-6XG6Y-4BT6T-R6H43` (Reminder: You will need BOTH 32-bit & 64-bit keys!)
8. Let setup do its thing, and when asked to restart, click `Don't Restart`, there is more the script has to do. The script will auto-restart for its 1st time when its done.
9. Don't walk away from your PC yet. In the middle of setup, you will need to do something. (You'll see). You should be at the low-color-depth screen that doesn't move text, after its done, that will be your 2nd reboot.
10. Once you get to the 3rd reboot, open a `Command Prompt` (`cmd`) Window RIGHT AWAY, by pressing & holding `SHIFT` + `F10` on your keyboard, until a window opens. Then type: `\a`, and then press `ENTER` on your keyboard. The `a.bat` script will delete a registry entry. Then click on the `X` on the top right of the `cmd.exe` window(s).
11. If asked to `Copy files from:`, click `Browse...`, open the folder on the root of your System Drive (again, usually `C:\`, and go to `$WIN_NT$.~LS` & then `amd64`, and click `Open`. Then, click `OK`.
12. Now, wait to be asked for another (2nd) product key. (Yes, I know, right!). 
13. Once asked for the (2nd) product key, type in your 64-Bit ISO Product Key, in my case, I will type in: `B76XF-FCR39-XBGFF-YMBFH-G6PMT`
14. There will be files (like `msdadc.dll`, that existed in 32-bit Windows Sever 2003, that don't work or exist in 64-bit Windows Sever 2003. If you are asked for a missing file and cannot find it in the 64-bit ISO, click `Cancel` on those files. These most likely comes from the registry leftovers.
15. All done. If you got this far, congrats!
